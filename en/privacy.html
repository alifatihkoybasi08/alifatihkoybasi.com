Privacy Notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This notice has been prepared pursuant to Article 10 of the Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (“KVKK”) and the Communiqué on the Principles and Procedures to Be Followed in Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform, in order to inform visitors of the alifatihkoybasi.com website and users of its contact form. This English version is provided for convenience; in case of any discrepancy, the Turkish version prevails.

1. Data controller

Your personal data is processed by Ali Fatih Köybaşı (the “Data Controller”), acting as the data controller. You can contact the Data Controller at info@alifatihkoybasi.com.

2. Personal data processed

The following personal data is processed through the contact form:

  • Identity: your first name and surname.
  • Contact: your email address and, if you choose to share it, your phone number.
  • Request: the content of your message.
  • Consent: the record of the explicit consent you give when submitting the form.
  • Security: when you submit the form, technical data such as your IP address, browser and device information that hCaptcha processes to tell automated (bot) submissions apart.

Please do not include special categories of personal data, such as health information, religion or political opinions, in your message.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

  • Receiving, evaluating and responding to your message. Legal ground: the legitimate interest of the Data Controller (KVKK Art. 5/2-f); where your message concerns a business or service request, its direct relation to the establishment or performance of a contract (KVKK Art. 5/2-c).
  • Protecting the form against automated (bot) and unwanted submissions. Legal ground: the legitimate interest of the Data Controller (KVKK Art. 5/2-f).
  • Establishing, exercising or protecting a right in the event of a dispute. Legal ground: KVKK Art. 5/2-e.
  • Responding to lawful requests of competent public authorities. Legal ground: compliance with a legal obligation of the Data Controller (KVKK Art. 5/2-ç).

4. Method of collection

Your personal data is collected electronically when you complete and submit the contact form on the website. Security data is collected automatically by hCaptcha when you submit the form.

5. Transfers, including transfers abroad

The information you submit through the form, and the security data generated when you submit it, are transferred to the following service providers, solely for the purposes of delivering your message to the Data Controller, protecting the form against abuse and responding to you. The servers of these providers are located outside Türkiye:

  • Web3Forms: the form service that forwards the information in the form to the Data Controller by email.
  • Google (Google Workspace): the email service where the message is received and the correspondence is conducted.
  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc., USA): the security service that protects the form against automated submissions; it is only activated when you submit the form.

This transfer is based on the explicit consent you give when submitting the form (KVKK Art. 9/6-a). Unless you give explicit consent, your message is not sent through the form.

Possible risks: The countries to which your data is transferred may not offer a level of personal data protection equivalent to that in Türkiye; the competent authorities of those countries may access the data under their own laws, and it may be more difficult for you to exercise your rights in those countries.

Your personal data may also be transferred to competent public authorities and judicial bodies where there is a legal obligation, limited to what is requested.

6. Retention period

Your personal data is retained for as long as necessary to respond to your request and thereafter for a reasonable period required in view of potential disputes; once the purpose of processing no longer exists, it is deleted, destroyed or anonymised. Copies held by the form service provider Web3Forms are automatically and permanently deleted after 7 days.

7. Hosting, cookies and links

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. (USA). GitHub processes technical data of visitors, such as IP addresses, for the security of its service and its own legal obligations under its own privacy statement; the Data Controller has no access to these records. The website itself does not use cookies and does not keep visitor statistics. hCaptcha, which is activated when you submit the form, may use cookies and similar technologies on its own domain for bot detection.

This site is protected by hCaptcha and its Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

The social media links on the website are plain links; no content from these platforms is displayed on the website and no data is shared with them until you click a link. Once you click a link, the privacy policy of the respective platform applies.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you have the right to apply to the Data Controller to:

  • a) learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • b) request information if your personal data has been processed,
  • c) learn the purpose of the processing and whether your data is used in line with that purpose,
  • ç) know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your personal data is transferred,
  • d) request the rectification of your personal data if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • e) request the erasure or destruction of your personal data under the conditions set out in Article 7 of KVKK,
  • f) request that the operations carried out under (d) and (e) be notified to the third parties to whom your personal data has been transferred,
  • g) object to a result to your detriment arising from the analysis of the processed data exclusively by automated systems,
  • ğ) claim compensation for damages arising from the unlawful processing of your personal data.

9. How to apply

You may submit your requests, in accordance with the Communiqué on the Principles and Procedures for Applications to the Data Controller, to info@alifatihkoybasi.com from the email address you previously provided to the Data Controller and that is registered in the Data Controller's system. Your application must include your first name and surname; your Turkish identity number if you are a Turkish citizen, or your nationality and passport number or, if any, identity number if you are a foreign national; your residential or business address for notifications; your email address for notifications, if any; your telephone number; and the subject of your request.

Your application will be concluded free of charge as soon as possible and within thirty days at the latest, depending on the nature of the request; where the process requires an additional cost, a fee may be charged according to the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board. If your application is rejected, you find the response insufficient or no response is given in time, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board under Article 14 of KVKK.

You may withdraw the explicit consent you gave when submitting the form at any time in the same way; the withdrawal does not have retroactive effect.
